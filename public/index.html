About Me

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student at the American University of Beirut, also pursuing coursework toward a B.S. in Mathematics; the dual degree isn't formally declared yet. I started programming at 11, and spent my early teens building small Android apps before moving into web development and, more recently, machine learning.

Most of my time outside coursework goes to Deafeat, a project I founded that combines a hardware device with a machine learning model to translate sign language into speech and back, enabling two-way communication between deaf and hearing users. I also cofounded FindVolunteering, a platform connecting NGOs and schools with volunteers, and I'm building Purpigal, a decision-support tool for business analysis.

I read widely on math, physics, and business, and I'm especially drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of theory and working software.
