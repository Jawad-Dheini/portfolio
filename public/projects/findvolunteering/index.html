FindVolunteering

A platform connecting NGOs, schools, and companies with volunteers through SDG-aligned opportunities.

Active Cofounder Jun 2025 – Present

Overview

FindVolunteering matches NGOs, schools, and companies with volunteers through opportunities aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The platform includes event management, QR-code-based attendance tracking, and analytics dashboards that let institutions measure social impact and volunteer engagement.

My Role

I cofounded the project and work on the product alongside my cofounder. Together we presented the initiative to the Lebanese Minister of Education, and secured paid interest from the Chair of the Association of IB World Schools in Lebanon.

Features

  • Matching NGOs, schools, and companies with volunteers
  • Event management tools for organizers
  • QR-code-based attendance tracking
  • Analytics dashboards for measuring social impact and volunteer engagement
